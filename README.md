**GroSwift App – An Online Grocery Delivery System**
The GroSwift App is a modern online grocery delivery application designed for customers in Lahore, Pakistan. The app allows users to conveniently shop for fresh fruits, vegetables, everyday groceries, bakery items, beverages, and farm-fresh products from the comfort of their homes.Users can sign up using their email, log in to their accounts, and manage their profiles with ease. The app offers an intuitive interface that includes a search bar for quick product searches, a favorites feature for saving preferred items, and an add-to-cart system for a smooth shopping experience.The GroSwift App focuses on freshness, usability, and fast delivery, ensuring that customers receive their grocery items quickly and safely anywhere within Lahore.
